What This Means for Realocate
US is one destination. The top 3 origin corridors alone generate ~87,000 permanent immigrant arrivals per year to the US. Each one is a person who needs to open a bank account, get a SIM, navigate residency paperwork, and find housing — without an agency holding their hand.
The real wedge is self-serve. Most of these migrants do not have employer-sponsored relocation services. B2B relocation agencies serve the top 5–10% of corporate assignees. The remaining 90% improvise — via WhatsApp groups, Facebook communities, and five browser tabs open at once. That is exactly who Realocate serves at $29.
COVID validated demand resilience. Even at the pandemic floor (FY2020–21), 10–12k visas/yr per corridor were issued. The market doesn't disappear — it compresses and then rebounds sharply. Philippines rebounded to near pre-COVID levels by FY2023–24. Migration is structural, not cyclical.
Phase 3 corridor expansion. These are US-destination corridors only. Global corridors (India→UK, Philippines→UAE, China→Canada, etc.) multiply the SAM by 5–10x. Realocate's architecture — corridor-filtered task templates + LLM fallback — is already built to support expansion with minimal incremental cost.
